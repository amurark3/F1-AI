## 3. Decisions (made; don't re-decide)

- **D1 f1db only.** This is a history page; f1db flags a title in the release that publishes the clinching round, and the Standings page already shows the computed (provisional) clinch in between. Every running season is labelled "after round N of M", so a lag is never presented as current.
- **D2 A clinched title counts** in the hero, the leaderboards and the wall: it is mathematically certain and f1db counts it. Its **points are never final**: they read "after round N of M", and the hero says how many rounds are still to run.
- **D3 Age is measured on the date of the race that decided the title** (`drivers_championship_decider`); it reproduces the plan's 20 y 68 d / 23 y 134 d.
- **D4 The hero is the newest season whose drivers' title is decided**; an open season never takes the hero.
- **D5 An open season on the wall** reads "Open" in the caption tone, with no bar and no gold (a leader is not a champion).
- **D6 The wall's bar** is the constructors' champion's colour only when that title is decided; historic teams get `NEUTRAL_TEAM_COLOR` through `getTeamColor` (the mockup's Lotus gold is invented, and gold means titles).
- **D7 Leaderboards show the top 8, widened to keep a tie at the cut whole**: 11 drivers (≥ 3) and 9 constructors (≥ 2). Cutting inside a tie would show Brabham and Stewart and hide Senna, Piquet and Lauda.
- **D8 Computed card titles use digits** ("Hamilton and Schumacher, 7 each", "Ferrari lead with 16"); no number-words helper exists. Names joined with `Intl.ListFormat` (standard library).
- **D9 Drop the mockup's "Mercedes can add a ninth in São Paulo"**: stale (f1db counts the ninth) and the clinch calculator's job.
- **D10 No page-level awaits** (PLAN Loading rule, every rebuilt page): pages pass promises; each section `use()`s its seed behind its own Suspense + `useSection`, like `standings/`. P3.6 then has nothing left for champions.
- **D11 Season page: no mockup**, so §5.5 specifies it from kit parts only. Its `generateMetadata` awaits nothing (static per year) and an impossible year 404s by a range check, because metadata must not hold the stream.
- **D12 Wall links use `prefetch={false}`**: 77 in-viewport prefetches would each render a season page against the backend.
- **D13 The backend cache is keyed by the installed f1db version**: the refresh loop in `main` installs releases in a running process, and today's unkeyed `_cache` would never show a new title until a restart (`current_standings.py` already keys by version).
- **D14 `is_in_progress` keeps its meaning** (drivers' title undecided) for compatibility; the new `rounds_completed` says whether the season still runs. Document both.
- **D15 No `StateTag`**: it lives in `grand-prix/` and a parallel task may move it; states go in text.

## 4. Backend

### 4.1 `app/data/champions.py`
- `_cache: dict[tuple[str | None, str], object]`; `def _key(name: str) -> tuple[str | None, str]: return (installed_version(), name)` (import `installed_version` from `app.data.f1db_source`). Every `_cache.get`/set uses `_key("list")`, `_key(f"detail:{year}")`, `_key("stats")`, `_key("latest")`. One-line why in a comment (D13).
- `_round_counts(conn, year) -> tuple[int, int]`: `SELECT COUNT(*), COALESCE(SUM(EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM race_data rd WHERE rd.race_id = r.id AND rd.type = 'RACE_RESULT')), 0) FROM race r WHERE r.year = ?` → `(round_count, rounds_completed)`. List rows use it (replacing the inline count) and gain `"rounds_completed"`; season detail gains `"round_count"` and `"rounds_completed"`.
- `_decider(conn, year) -> dict | None`: `SELECT r.round, r.date, ci.place_name FROM race r LEFT JOIN circuit ci ON ci.id = r.circuit_id WHERE r.year = ? AND r.drivers_championship_decider = 1` → `{"round", "date", "place"}`. Season detail gains `"decided_at": _decider(conn, year)`.
- `_age(born: date, on: date) -> dict[str, int]`, exactly:
  ```python
  years = on.year - born.year - ((on.month, on.day) < (born.month, born.day))
  year = born.year + years
  leap_day = (born.month, born.day) == (2, 29) and not calendar.isleap(year)
  birthday = date(year, 3, 1) if leap_day else born.replace(year=year)
  return {"years": years, "days": (on - birthday).days}
  ```
- `latest_champion() -> dict`, cached under `_key("latest")`. One query, oldest first:
  `SELECT sds.year, d.id, d.name, d.last_name, d.date_of_birth, co.name AS nationality, r.round, r.date, ci.place_name FROM season_driver_standing sds JOIN driver d ON d.id = sds.driver_id LEFT JOIN country co ON co.id = d.nationality_country_id LEFT JOIN race r ON r.year = sds.year AND r.drivers_championship_decider = 1 LEFT JOIN circuit ci ON ci.id = r.circuit_id WHERE sds.championship_won = 1 ORDER BY sds.year`.
  No rows → return `{"error": "No decided drivers' title in f1db"}` (not cached). Else `latest = rows[-1]`, `earlier = rows[:-1]`, and return exactly these keys:
  - `season`, `name`, `last_name`, `nationality`;
  - `decided_at`: `{round, date, place}` or `None` when the latest title has no decider row;
  - `round_count`, `rounds_completed`: `_round_counts` for that season;
  - `age`: `_age(dob, decider date)`, or `None` without a date of birth or a decider;
  - `previous_youngest`: among `earlier` rows that have both dates, the one with the fewest days lived at its decider → `{season, name, last_name, age}`, else `None`;
  - `youngest_ever`: `age is not None and previous_youngest is not None` and the latest title's days lived < that one's;
  - `previous_compatriot`: the last `earlier` row with the same non-null `nationality` and a different driver id → `{season, name, last_name}`, else `None`;
  - `title_number`: rows with the latest driver's id; `champion_number`: distinct driver ids over all rows.
- Module docstring, "Key f1db facts": `championship_won` turns 1 as soon as a title is mathematically decided, rounds left or not (2026: Antonelli after round 19 of 23). `is_in_progress` means the drivers' title is undecided; `rounds_completed < round_count` means the season still runs.
