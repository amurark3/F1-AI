Global chrome · P3.5a · restart lights

Five lights in the top bar, only while the server restarts

The full-screen warm-up overlay goes. When /api/ready fails, the bar's status slot shows five start lights, lit by the backend's real warm-up stage, on every route. The page under it stays usable: nothing is inert, scroll is never locked and focus never moves.

Decision · placement

The bar carries the lights; a card appears only for the buttons

Starting and back online live in the top-bar slot at every width, phones included. The lamps and a short label fit the slot at 390 px, and the backend's stage detail sits in the kit Tooltip (hover, focus or tap). A notice that never covers content never needs dismissing.

Unreachable adds one small bottom card (bottom-right on desktop, full width on phones), because Try again and Dismiss need 44 px targets the 60 px bar can't hold on a phone, and actions shouldn't hide behind a tooltip. The card is not a dialog: it takes no focus, blocks nothing and goes with Dismiss.

For P3.5b: on phones the slot is normally hidden (hidden sm:flex; the race chip lives in the drawer). The restart lights are the one exception that shows in the phone bar.

  • Never a blocking overlay
  • No inert, no scroll lock
  • Lights from real stages, no timer
  • No glow, no loop
  • role="status" · "Server status"

Server isn't responding

No answer for 2 minutes. It may be redeploying or down. Pages stay usable, and sections that need data offer their own retry.

  • After the click
  • Try again closes the card and restarts the probe; the bar goes back to "Server starting" with the lamps at the count already reached.
  • Dismiss removes the card and the bar strip for this run; the slot falls back to the race chip (empty on /). A later restart shows again.
  • No focus moves. The card is reachable by Tab after the page content; it isn't a dialog and nothing behind it is inert.
  • Red means stopped, said in words too. "Not responding" is --rc-red-text; the lamps stay --f1-red fills.
