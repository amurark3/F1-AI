A setup change under parc fermé without approval means a pit-lane start

Once the car is under parc fermé, its setup can only change with the Technical Delegate's approval 1. In Mexico City 2026, Racing Bulls changed the suspension setup of car 30 without that approval. The stewards found breaches of Articles B3.4.3, B3.5.3 and B3.5.4, and required the car to start the race from the pit lane 2.

1

FIA F1 Regulations · Articles B3.5.3, B3.5.4 · as applied in decision 65Changing a car's setup under parc fermé without the Technical Delegate's approval is not in accordance with these articles.

2

Stewards' decision · Mexico City GP 2026 · Document 65"Required to start the Race from the pit lane." Car 30's suspension setup was changed under parc fermé before the start, without the approval of the Technical Delegate.
