## 1. Goal and scope

Rebuild `/race-control/rulebook` to `rulebook.html`:

- a `PageHero` titled "Ask the rulebook";
- a large search box with a red Search button;
- four quick questions that submit at once;
- an answer card: the eyebrow, an **AI-written heading and paragraph** with numbered source chips, the numbered sources, and the note.

**Evidence, chosen by code:**

1. the top three regulation passages from the pgvector search, after the cross-encoder rerank;
2. the real stewards' decisions from the D2 store (`fia_documents`) that cite an article those passages name, each linking its FIA PDF.

**The model** (AMD Token Lab, then Gemini, through `llm.py`) sees only that evidence and writes the heading and the paragraph. Code checks the reply. When the model fails, or its reply fails the check, the card shows a computed heading and paragraph, labelled as computed. A checked reply is cached per normalised question and evidence, so the quick questions don't spend the Gemini free quota.

**When the rule index is off** (production on Render today, where `ENABLE_LOCAL_MODELS` must stay `false`: the embedding model doesn't fit in 512 MB):

- the passage half is the kit failure card "Regulation passages unavailable", with the honest reason;
- below it, the stewards' decisions that share the question's words are found by **keyword** search, labelled as keyword matches and not article matches, with computed text and no LLM call.

The D2 store is live in production: the ingest workflow fills it from `main`, and `PostgresFiaStore` creates its table on first use.

**Removed:**
- the category select;
- the "Regulation Sources" panel;
- `WorkspaceSplit`, `SectionHeader`, `SectionLoader`, `InlineNotice`, `StatusPill`, `rcFont` and `lucide-react` from this folder;
- every hex literal and `#00FF78`;
- the boilerplate "Found N cited regulation excerpts…" text;
- the backend's `fallback_rulebook_search`, with its invented placeholder citation;
- the `error` field that sent `str(exc)` to the client.

**Out of scope:**
- `consult_rulebook` and the Engineer;
- PDF links for regulation passages (the corpus holds file names, not URLs);
- `SourceBadge` (P7.5);
- a `?q=` URL state;
- any e2e spec;
- a repair round after a failed check: it would spend quota; the computed text is the fallback.

## 2. Backend

### 2.1 `POST /api/race-control/rulebook/search` (`app/api/routers/race_control.py`)

- **Body:** `RulebookSearchRequest` (`app/api/schemas/race_control.py`): `query: str = Field(min_length=1, max_length=300)`, plus `category` and `year` as today (the page sends neither). An empty or over-long `query` gets a 422.
- **Handler:** `return await asyncio.to_thread(rulebook.search_rulebook, request.query, request.category, request.year)`. The echo of `query`, `category` and `year` goes: the payload carries its own.

**Payload** (every key is always present):

```jsonc
{
  "query": "When is a driver required to start from the pit lane?",  // stripped
  "year": 2026,                       // resolve_rulebook_year(year)
  "status": "answered",               // "answered" | "keyword" | "unavailable"
  "reason": null,                     // keyword: why the regulations weren't searched; unavailable: why nothing; answered: null
  "articles": [                       // 1–3 when answered, else []
    { "cite": 1,
      "codes": ["B3.5.2", "B3.5.3", "B3.4.2", "B3.4.3", "B3.5.4"],  // article_codes(excerpt), in order, deduped

    "match": "article",               // "article" (answered) | "keyword" (keyword)
    "status": "matched",              // "matched" | "none" | "unavailable" (store failed; answered mode only)

    "total": 1,                       // all matches
    "articles": ["B3.4.3", "B3.5.3", "B3.5.4"],  // article mode: ordered union of every match's `matched`; keyword mode: []
    "words": [],                      // keyword mode: the question's search words (§2.4); article mode: []
    "items": [                        // at most DECISIONS_SHOWN = 3
      { "cite": 4,                    // continues after the articles; keyword mode starts at 1
        "number": 65, "event": "Mexico City Grand Prix",
```

### 2.2 `app/services/rulebook.py` (rewrite; keep `resolve_rulebook_year` as it is): orchestration

Constants: `PAGE_EXCERPTS = 3`. Keep the over-fetch `RULEBOOK_TOP_K * 4`.

**`search_rulebook(query, category=None, year=None) -> dict`:**

1. **Blank after strip:** `unavailable`, reason "Enter a question to search the regulations." (`decisions` and `summary` null).
2. **Find why the regulations can't be searched,** if they can't. Each reason goes to the keyword path (step 4):
   - **`not RULEBOOK_ENABLED`:** "The regulation index isn't set up on this server, so the regulations were not searched."
   - **`not ENABLE_LOCAL_MODELS`:** "Regulation search is off on this server: it needs a model this server doesn't have the memory to run. The regulations were not searched."
   - **`rulebook_search(…, raise_errors=True)` or `rerank` raises:** log `rulebook.search.failed` with `error=str(exc)`; reason "The regulation search failed, so the regulations were not searched. Try again shortly." No exception text reaches the client. This is the module's only `except Exception`.
   - **No hits:** `f"The FIA {year} F1 Regulations aren't in the search index yet."` (pgvector always returns nearest neighbours when the year has chunks).
3. **Answered:**
   - `articles` from `rerank(query, candidates, PAGE_EXCERPTS)`, cites 1…n;
   - `decisions = rulebook_decisions.matching(year, [a["codes"] for a in articles], first_cite=n + 1)`;
   - `summary = rulebook_answer.write_summary(query, year, articles, decisions)`;
   - `status: "answered"`, `reason: null`.
4. **Keyword:** `decisions = rulebook_decisions.keyword_matching(year, query)`.
   - If it raises `FiaStoreError`: `unavailable`, reason = the step 2 reason + " The stewards' decisions could not be read either." (`decisions` and `summary` null).
   - Otherwise: `status: "keyword"`, `reason` = the step 2 reason, `articles: []`, `summary = rulebook_text.keyword_summary(year, decisions)` (computed, `why: null`).

**Article fields:**
- **`source`:** with `_SECTION = re.compile(r"Section ([A-F]) \[([^\]]+)\]")` on the file name, `f"Section {letter} · {name}"`. Otherwise `f"{metadata type or 'Regulatory'} Regulations"`.
- **`page`:** `int(page) + 1` when it is an int or float, else `None`.
- **`excerpt`:** `" ".join(page_content.split())`.

### 2.3 `app/services/rulebook_decisions.py` (new): matching by article number

- **`ARTICLE_CODE = re.compile(r"(?<![\w.])[A-F]\d{1,2}(?:\.\d{1,2}){1,3}[a-z]?(?!\w)")`** and `article_codes(text) -> tuple[str, ...]` (in order, deduped).
  - Over the 2026 Sporting PDF it finds 457 codes; it finds no "Appendix B2" and no bare "B3".
- **`cites(decision_article, code) -> bool`:** true when `decision_article == code`, or when `decision_article` starts with `code` and the next character is `.` or a lowercase letter.
  - Matches: "B1.6.3a" ⊂ "B1.6.3", "B1.6.2b.i" ⊂ "B1.6.2", "B3.5.3" ⊂ "B3.5".
  - Never matches: "B1.62" vs "B1.6", or any "ISC …" article.
- **Season index** (`_season(year)`, `TtlCache[int, _Season]`, `SEASON_TTL_SECONDS = 600.0`; a raised `FiaStoreError` is not cached):
  - Steps:
    1. Read `store.events(year)`.
    2. For each event, sorted, read `store.load_event(year, event)`.
    3. Keep a document only when it meets all four rules:
       - `withdrawn is None`;
       - `text` is set;
       - `document_kind(title) == KIND_PENALTY`;
       - `parse_stewards_decision(title, text)` succeeds (skip on `DocumentParseError`) with `status == STATUS_DECIDED` (no summonses).
    4. The number is `parsed.document or title_number(title)`. Skip a document with neither.
  - Hold the §2.1 item fields and `len(events)`.
- **`matching(year, codes_per_article, first_cite) -> dict`** (`match: "article"`):
  - On `FiaStoreError`: log `rulebook.decisions.unavailable` and return status `unavailable` with `events: None`, `total: 0` and empty lists.
  - A decision matches when any of its articles `cites` any passage code. `matched` = its articles that cite some code, in its own order.
  - Order: by the lowest passage index it matches, then newest first by `published` parsed as `%d.%m.%y %H:%M` (an unparseable time sorts last). Show `DECISIONS_SHOWN = 3`.
- **`keyword_matching(year, query) -> dict`** is §2.4. It raises `FiaStoreError` to the caller.

### 2.4 Keyword matching (same module), used only when the rule index is off

- **`search_words(text) -> tuple[str, ...]`** (ordered, deduped), the same for the question and for a decision:
  1. Fold accents (NFKD, drop combining marks) and lowercase.
  2. Replace `\bpu\b` with "power unit" (the FIA's titles say "PU elements"; the one synonym).
  3. Split on `[^a-z0-9]+`, and drop words shorter than 2 characters.
  4. Drop `STOP_WORDS` = a, an, the, is, are, was, were, be, been, do, does, did, how, what, when, where, which, who, whom, why, can, could, should, would, will, may, might, must, if, of, in, on, at, to, for, from, by, with, as, and, or, not, no, it, its, this, that, these, those, there, their, they, them, i, me, we, you, my, our, your, about, under, during, after, before, any, mean, means, rule, rules, regulation, regulations, f1, formula, driver, drivers, car, cars, team, teams, race, count, counts, work, works, happen, happens, get, gets, allowed.
  5. Stem: drop one trailing "s" from a word longer than 3 characters that doesn't end in "ss" ("limits" → "limit", "points" → "point").
- **A decision's words** = `search_words(f"{offence} {decision}")`, only the title's offence and the decision line (the document's boilerplate would match everything).
- **Score** = the number of the question's words found in the decision's words.
  - A decision matches when its score is at least `max(1, ceil(len(question_words) / 2))`.
  - Rank by score (high first), then newest first. `matched` = the shared words in the question's order. Show `DECISIONS_SHOWN`, cite from 1.
  - Return `match: "keyword"`, `words` = the question's words, `articles: []`.
  - Status is `matched` or `none`. No words at all → `none`, with `words: []`.
- **Expected on the real R19 documents** (assert in tests; if reality differs, report it, don't bend the rule):
  - "When is a driver required to start from the pit lane?" → words `required, start, pit, lane`; first item document 65 (score 4); the pit-lane speeding decisions follow with score 2.
  - "What is a power-unit grid penalty?" → `power, unit, grid, penalty`; first item document 17 (PU elements, a 50-place drop).
  - "How do penalty points work?" → `penalty, point`; first items documents 84 and 85 (each "… penalty points").
  - "What counts as track limits?" → `track, limit`; includes documents 69 and 76 ("…the track…").

### 2.5 `app/services/rulebook_answer.py` (new): the AI-written heading and paragraph

**`write_summary(query, year, articles, decisions) -> dict`** returns the §2.1 `summary`.

1. **Cache key:** `sha256` of `json.dumps({"q": normalise(query), "year": year, "evidence": [...]}, sort_keys=True)`.
   - `normalise` = lowercase, collapse whitespace, strip trailing `?!.` and spaces.
   - `evidence` = for each article `[cite, document, page, sha256(excerpt)]`, and for each decision item `[cite, url]`.
   - A new decision, a changed passage or a re-ingested document therefore asks the model again, while the same quick question over the same evidence never does.
2. **`analysis_cache.get("rulebook_answer", key, ANSWER_VERSION)`** (`app/data/analysis_cache.py`'s `analysis_cache`: Postgres `analysis_cache` table plus bounded memory; memory only without `DATABASE_URL`). A hit returns it.
   - `ANSWER_VERSION = 1`: bump it whenever the prompt or the check changes.
   - Only checked AI replies are stored. A computed fallback is never stored, so the next ask tries the model again.
   - Why this table: it is keyed and versioned, persists across restarts, is already bounded in memory, and holds no growing whole-document blob (`app_documents` would).
3. **Ask the model.** For `provider in available_providers()`, call `build_chat_llm(provider).invoke(messages)`.
   - On an exception with `is_provider_failure(exc)`: `mark_unavailable(provider, exc)` and try the next one.
   - Any other exception, or `RuntimeError` from `available_providers()` (no key), stops: `why = "ai_unavailable"`. Log `rulebook.answer.llm_failed` with the provider and the error type.
   - Read the reply with `message_text`.
4. **Parse:** strip a leading and trailing code fence, then `json.loads`. It must be an object with string `heading` (1–120 characters, no `[` and no `*`) and string `paragraph` (1–1,200 characters).
5. **Check** (`check_reply(heading, paragraph, articles, decisions) -> list[str]` returns the problems; empty = pass; pure, unit-tested on its own). This is the claim checker's idea (a claim is checked only against the rows the answer was built from), applied to this evidence.
   - **Source markers:** every `[n]` in the paragraph is the `cite` of an article or decision in the evidence, and there is at least one.
   - **Article numbers:** every match of `ARTICLE_CODE` in heading and paragraph equals a code of the evidence (any article's `codes` or any decision's `articles`), or is a parent of one (`cites(evidence_code, claimed)`). So "B3.5" passes when "B3.5.3" is in the evidence, and "B3.5.9" fails.
   - **"Article N.N…" numbers** (`Article\s+(\d+(?:\.\d+)+)`, the ISC form): each must appear inside a decision's `articles` string.
   - **Document numbers** (`\b(?:document|doc\.?)\s*(?:no\.?\s*)?(\d{1,3})\b`, any case): each is a decision item's `number`.
   - **Car numbers** (`\bcar\s+(?:no\.?\s*)?(\d{1,2})\b`, any case): each is a decision item's `car`.
   - **Bold:** at most three `**…**` spans, balanced.
   - Add a code comment: `# shortcut: penalty values, names and times are not checked; upgrade by checking every number against the evidence if a bad one is seen.`
6. **Outcome:**
   - Pass: `{heading, paragraph, written_by: "ai", why: null}`; put it in the cache.
   - Fail (parse or check): log `rulebook.answer.check_failed` with the problems; `why = "check_failed"`.
   - Either failure: `rulebook_text.computed_summary(year, articles, decisions)` with that `why`.

**Messages:**
- **System prompt** (a module constant): "You answer questions about the FIA Formula 1 regulations using only the numbered sources below. Reply with one JSON object and nothing else: {"heading": "...", "paragraph": "..."}. heading: one plain sentence of at most 100 characters that answers the question, with no source numbers and no asterisks. paragraph: at most 120 words; after each claim put the number of the source that supports it in square brackets, like [1]; cite at least one source. Name an article, document or car number only as it appears in the sources. Do not add penalties, numbers, names, dates or events the sources do not state. If the sources do not answer the question, say so plainly in both. You may mark up to three key phrases with **double asterisks**."
- **Human message:** `Question: {query}`, then `Sources:`, then one block per source:
  - an article: `[n] FIA {year} F1 Regulations, {source}, page {page}, articles {codes}:\n{excerpt}`;
  - a decision: `[n] Stewards' decision, {event} {year}, document {number}, car {car}{ (driver)}, articles {articles}, offence: {offence}, decision: "{decision}"`.
- The model never sees a keyword match: keyword mode makes no call.

### 2.6 `app/services/rulebook_text.py` (new): the computed text (also the fallback)

The output uses the same markup as the model (`[n]`, `**…**`), so the frontend has one renderer.

- **Helpers:**
  - **`article_label(codes)`:** `[]` → `None`; "Article B3.5.3"; "Articles B3.5.3 and B3.5.4"; "Articles X, Y and Z"; more than 3 → "Articles X, Y, Z and N more".
  - **`where(article)`:** with codes, `f"{article_label(codes)} ({source}, page {page})"`; without codes, `f"{source}, page {page}"`. Drop ", page …" when the page is null.
- **`computed_summary(year, articles, decisions)`** (answered mode):
  - **Heading:** with codes on article 1, "The closest rule: " + `article_label`; otherwise "The closest passage: " + source (+ ", page N").
  - **Paragraph, sentence 1:** "The closest passage in the FIA {year} F1 Regulations is **{where(article 1)}** [1]." With more articles, add " Related passages: [2] [3]."
  - **Paragraph, sentence 2,** by `decisions.status`:
    - `unavailable`: "The stewards' decisions could not be read just now, so none is cited."
    - `none`, no article has codes: "These passages name no article number, so no stewards' decision can be matched to them."
    - `none`, `events == 0`: "No {year} stewards' decisions have been collected yet."
    - `none`: "None of the stewards' decisions collected from {events} {year} event(s) cites {article_label(union of all codes)}."
    - `matched`: "The stewards cited {article_label(decisions.articles)} in {total} decision(s) from the {events} {year} event(s) collected." When `total > len(items)`, add " {len(items)} of them are cited below." Then " At the {event}, document {number}, car {car}{ (driver)}: **“{decision}”** [cite]", with no full stop after the marker.
- **`keyword_summary(year, decisions)`** (keyword mode):
  - **Heading:** `total > 0` → "{total} stewards' decision(s) share words with the question"; otherwise "No stewards' decision shares words with the question".
  - **Paragraph:**
    - no words: "The question has no word to search the stewards' decisions by."
    - `events == 0`: "No {year} stewards' decisions have been collected yet."
    - `none`: "None of the stewards' decisions collected from {events} {year} event(s) mentions {words joined with ', '}."
    - `matched`: "Found by the words {words joined with ', '} in the stewards' decisions collected from {events} {year} event(s), not matched to an article of the regulations. The closest: at the {event}, document {number}, car {car}{ (driver)}: **“{decision}”** [1]". When `total > len(items)`, add " {len(items)} of {total} are cited below."
