Why did Antonelli lose the lead in Mexico?

Engineer · Mexico City GP

He led until he pitted for mediums on lap 43. The Safety Car came out on lap 44 and the race was red-flagged on lap 45. That handed the cars still on track a cheap stop, then a free tyre change under the suspension. Verstappen, who stopped on lap 27, led from lap 44 and kept the lead at the standing restart. Antonelli finished third, which was enough to clinch the title.
