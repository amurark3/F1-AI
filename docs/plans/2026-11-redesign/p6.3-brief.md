## 1. Goal and scope

Rebuild `/race-control/power-units` to `power-units.html`: a Barlow `PageHero`, a KPI row, the allocation pip matrix for every car in championship order with shared-seat tags, the "Who paid the most" grid-place ranking with the shared-seat car card, and the source note.
